Privacy Policy – Benefit PR TRACK
Last Updated: November 2026

Benefit PR TRACK is an internal enterprise application developed exclusively for authorized employees of our company to track and manage internal Material Requests (MR) and procurement workflows.

1. Data Collection and Usage:
The application collects only essential business and account information (such as employee name, corporate email/username, role, and purchase request activity) strictly required for internal operational tracking and authentication.

2. Push Notifications:
Device tokens are used solely to deliver internal workflow status notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and Apple Push Notification service (APNs).

3. Data Sharing & Security:
We do not sell, rent, or share any user or company data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. All data is securely stored and accessible only to authorized company personnel.
